Aloe Vera (Aloe barbadensis miller)

Aloe Vera is a succulent known for its healing gel and minimal care needs.

🌞 Light

Needs bright indirect light or filtered sunlight.

💧 Watering

Water every 2–3 weeks. Avoid overwatering.

🌡️ Temperature

Thrives at 18–30°C.

🪴 Potting

Use cactus/succulent soil. Pots must have drainage holes.

✨ Fun Facts

  • The gel is used for burns and skincare.
  • One of the oldest medicinal plants in history.
