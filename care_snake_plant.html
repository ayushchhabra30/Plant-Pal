Snake Plant (Sansevieria trifasciata)

The Snake Plant is nearly indestructible and ideal for beginners. It tolerates low light and irregular watering.

🌞 Light

Grows in low to bright indirect light. Extremely adaptable.

💧 Watering

Water every 2–3 weeks. Allow soil to fully dry.

🌡️ Temperature

Ideal: 15–30°C.

🪴 Potting

Use cactus/succulent mix. Repot every 2 years.

✨ Fun Facts

  • Releases oxygen at night — great bedroom plant.
  • Removes airborne toxins like benzene and formaldehyde.
