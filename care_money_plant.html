Money Plant (Epipremnum aureum)

The Money Plant is one of the easiest and most popular indoor plants. It is believed to bring positivity, good health, and financial luck.

🌞 Light

Thrives in **bright indirect light**, but also survives in low light.

💧 Watering

Water **once a week**. Let soil dry slightly between waterings.

🌡️ Temperature

Ideal temperature: **18–30°C**.

🪴 Potting

Grows well in soil OR water. Repot every 1–2 years.

✨ Fun Facts

  • Symbol of prosperity in Vastu & Feng Shui.
  • Very fast-growing indoor vine.
  • Purifies indoor air by removing toxins.
